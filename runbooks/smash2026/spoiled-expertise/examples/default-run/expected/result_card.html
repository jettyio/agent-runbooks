From the abstract

We advance a concept of spoiled expertise which takes distinct forms based on two dimensions: knowledge workers' stance of adoption of epistemic technology, and locus of evaluation of expertise. Together these two dimensions culminate into four forms of spoiled expertise: (1) slopped expertise, (2) lagging expertise, (3) shunned expertise, and (4) outdated expertise.
