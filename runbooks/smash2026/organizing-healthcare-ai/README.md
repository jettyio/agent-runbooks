# Beyond Algorithms: Organizing Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare Through Expertise, Governance, and Knowledge Infrastructures

Nasrin Sultana, Dhenuka Radhakrishnan, Jamie Strain · SMASH 2026 (Poster) · [Paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7608858)

> We develop a framework that conceptualizes sustainable deployment of AI as an emergent organizational property produced through expertise coordination, governance infrastructures, and knowledge infrastructures. Four propositions are advanced linking expertise coordination and organizational infrastructure to accountability and sustainability.

This runbook reruns part of the paper's analysis on Jetty in a few minutes. Change the inputs in `RUNBOOK.md`'s Parameters table to try your own.

## Default run

A generic assistant crossed 5 of 9 boundary probes, including clinical dosing. Designs with stated limits or a policy corpus crossed 0 of 9, and only corpus-equipped designs answered institutional questions correctly (100% vs 25%).

- The institute and its policies are fictional; no CHEO material was used.
- Nine probes, one response each.
