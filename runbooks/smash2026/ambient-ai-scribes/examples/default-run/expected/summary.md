Net minutes per note: generic +1.5 min saved, customized +3.3 min saved (vs 6-min baseline); omissions and format errors dominated errors in generic drafts.

Hours/month ladder: promise 4,550 h → pilot reality 6.6 h (MC 90%: 2.7–11.5) → +customization 14.2 h → +remove barriers 19.7 h → +all enrollees active 40.3 h → full adoption 2,178 h.
